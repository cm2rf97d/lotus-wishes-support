Your data stays with you

Privacy Policy

Lotus Wishes does not require an account. You can leave a wish or leave it blank.

Applies to Lotus Wishes 1.0 for iPhone · Updated October 4, 2026

The app does not upload your lotuses, wishes or usage records to the developer.

The app has no ads, third-party analytics or tracking, and no added crash-reporting service.

What is stored on your device

  • Folding progress and lotus designs in each mode.
  • Names and wishes you enter. These fields can be left blank.
  • The dates each lotus was saved and offered.
  • Your atmosphere, track, volume and playback preferences.

Drafts and saved lotuses are separate

Folding progress is saved automatically. After finishing, tap Save Lotus to add it to My Lotuses. Saved lotuses and each mode’s unfinished draft are stored separately.

Deleting a saved lotus does not clear the wish or progress in its draft. Starting a new lotus in the same mode replaces that mode’s draft after the confirmation flow. Your collection and other modes’ progress are kept.

Backups and changing phones

Depending on your device settings, data may be saved and restored through iCloud or computer backups. Lotus Wishes does not provide a separate account or cross-device syncing.

Whether backups are enabled, completed or restored depends on your device and backup settings. The app does not keep a separate copy that the developer can recover for you.

Managing and keeping data

You can edit the wish or delete a saved lotus from its details before offering it. Offered lotuses are kept as memories and cannot be edited or deleted within the app.

Delete App on iPhone removes the app’s data from that device. Offload App keeps its documents and data. Existing system backups are managed in your backup settings; deleting a lotus inside the app does not immediately remove it from those backups.

Music and external links

Music is bundled with the app and plays offline. Your wishes are not sent to Suno.

External websites open only when you tap the official chanting page, a music source or a license link. The app does not attach your lotus or wish to these links. External websites handle browsing data under their own privacy policies.

System services and this website

iOS backups and system diagnostics follow your Apple and device settings. Apple provides these system services.

These help pages include no ads, analytics, tracking scripts or external fonts.
